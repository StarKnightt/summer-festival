## Build

Bodies are metaball blobs (body / head / muzzle / legs / tail as ellipsoid elements) converted to a
mesh and decimated to ~2.2k triangles; ears, eyes, nose, mouth and whiskers are small pieces ray-cast
onto the real head surface and merged into the same skinned mesh. Fur patterns are per-face
materials. Skin weights are analytic: each metaball element is tagged with a bone and a vertex is
weighted by the elements whose field produced it (≤ 4 influences, normalized); face pieces and ears
are rigid to `head` / `ear_L` / `ear_R`.

## Nodes

Each cat is a top-level armature node (origin on the ground under the body, facing +Z in glTF) with
one skinned mesh child `<cat>_body`.

| node | look | size w×d×h (m) |
|---|---|---|
| `cat_sleep` | ginger tabby with cream muzzle / paws / tail tip, curled asleep, closed-arc eyes | 0.37 × 0.42 × 0.21 |
| `cat_sit` | calico (white with orange and black patches), sitting upright, green eyes, tail round the paws | 0.28 × 0.30 × 0.44 |
| `cat_loaf` | black loaf with white socks, amber eyes, tail along the right side | 0.27 × 0.44 × 0.29 |

Materials: `fur_ginger`, `fur_ginger_dark`, `fur_cream`, `fur_white`, `fur_orange`, `fur_black`,
`fur_black_soft` (muzzle tint), `fur_inner_ear`, `eye_dark`, `eye_green`, `eye_amber`, `eye_hilite`,
`face_pink` (nose), `face_line` (closed eyes, mouth, whiskers). Unknown `fur_` prefix → standard cel.

## Rig

Bones (same names in each cat): `root` → `body` → `head` → `ear_L`, `ear_R`; `body` → `tail1` →
`tail2` → `tail3`. `root` does not deform. **three.js de-duplicates node names per file**, so in the
second and third cat the bones load as `body_1`, `head_1` … / `body_2` …; animation tracks are
already bound to the renamed nodes by GLTFLoader, so `AnimationMixer(catRoot)` just works. Look
bones up inside a cat with `name.replace(/_\d+$/, '')` if you need them.

Attachment point for a petting hand / heart FX: bone `head`, about 6 cm above its head along +Y.
